The Networking Engine's Two Gears

Built on world-class events and Africa's sport tourism activation.

The Networking Engine operates through two precision-built Landing Gears — each one distinct in function, but deeply interconnected in purpose. Together, Event Management & Destination Activation and ASTDN form NSTEDPath's most powerful convening and commercial activation force.

Gear 1

MICE Checkpoint

Strategic gatherings that drive investment, build capacity, and launch global brands. From institutional summits to MICE strategy and experiential activation. Hosts 2 BINDU flagships (THAT'S World Networking Summit & APEX Africa), 2 ASTDN flagships (ASTNI & AESTA), plus conferences, workshops, and capacity building programs.

MICE Checkpoint

Strategic gatherings that convert relationships into commitments, and commitments into measurable destination growth.

Our MICE Checkpoint arm is the commercial accelerator of NSTEDPath — designing and executing world-class gatherings that create urgency, unlock deals, activate partnerships, and put destinations on the global map. These are the moments where the NSTEDPath flywheel accelerates: connecting the right people, driving immediate commercial outcomes, and generating the market signals that power the entire ecosystem.

N

Network - Forge the relationships

Forge Relationships

  • Institutional Summits: Facilitating high-stakes dialogue between governments and global capital — creating decision rooms where commitments are made and tourism economies move forward.
  • MICE Strategy & Execution: Positioning your destination as a world- class hub for Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions — the highest-yield segment in global tourism and a direct driver of revenue.
  • Trade & Investor Networking: Curated matchmaking between destination authorities, private investors, tour operators, and global travel trade buyers.
D

Develop - Build the capability

Build Capability

  • Knowledge Transfer: Masterclasses and workshops that convert insight into capability — bridging local talent with global standards and accelerating destination readiness.
  • Professional Training: Elevating the destination's service ecosystem through certified excellence programs that strengthen delivery, build confidence, and improve commercial performance.
  • Destination Strategy Workshops: Hands-on working sessions that translate NSTED Pulse intelligence into actionable destination development roadmaps.

Promote - Amplify to the world

Amplify to the World

  • Experiential Activation: Creating signature events that transform the destination's brand into a live market force — generating media, momentum, and commercial demand at once.
  • Media & Content Production: Capturing every event as premium content — reels, press coverage, and digital assets that extend the event's reach far beyond the room.
  • Campaign Launch Platforms: Using events as the launchpad for destination marketing campaigns, driving immediate awareness, bookings, and investor interest.
