Paradise Resort

Find your calm

Welcome to Paradise Resort, where the mountains' river road and sunshine meet. We are conveniently located in the Zambezi Escarpment, along the marvellous Mavhuradonha mountain range to the East and the vast Dande Safari Resorts to the West. Approximately 209km from the Capital city Harare, along A12 road connecting Muzarabani with Mahuhwe, Mozambique and Zambia

A Stay That Stays With You

At Paradise Resort, it's not just about where you sleep—it's about how you feel while you're here. Whether you're unwinding in one of our beautifully designed rooms, dining with ocean views, or exploring nearby trails and beaches, every detail is designed to bring you closer to the things that matter most.

Here's what awaits you:

  • Nature All Around - Surrounded by beauty, from lush greenery to breathtaking sunsets.
  • Modern Comfort - Spacious rooms, cozy beds, and a touch of island elegance.
  • Taste of Paradise - Locally inspired cuisine served fresh, daily.
  • Wellness & Tranquility - Spa treatments, yoga, and peaceful spaces to recharge.
  • Explore More - From ocean adventures to local cultural experiences, find your perfect pace.
