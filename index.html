Five Corridor Classes — Where Keys Are Issued

Every corridor key in NSTEDPath belongs to one of five classes, each defining what a key looks like, what it means to clear it, and what constraint keeps it live.

Sport Corridor

Keys follow the format [region]- sport-[function] and clear when a hosting mandate, venue readiness score, and airlift access are all confirmed. The binding constraint is hosting mandate expiry.

Water Basin Corridor

Keys follow the format [basin]- water-[basin-id] and clear once a multi-sovereign access agreement, environmental baseline, and demand signal are in place. The binding constraint is sovereign agreement renewal.
