Begin Your Journey With Excellence

At Rio Tinto Zhome High School, we don’t just admit students — we welcome future leaders, innovators, and change-makers. Our admissions process is designed to identify potential, nurture talent, and build confident young individuals prepared for tomorrow.

Why Apply To Our School?

Academic Excellence

Consistently strong results supported by experienced educators and structured learning systems.

Holistic Development

Sports, leadership programs, clubs, and co-curricular activities that build confidence and character.

Supportive Environment

A safe and nurturing community that prioritizes student wellbeing and personal growth.

Our Admission Process

01

Submit Application

Complete and submit the official application form with all required documentation.

02

Entrance Assessment

Applicants may sit for an academic assessment based on grade level.

03

Interview

A meeting with parents and school leadership to align expectations and goals.

04

Enrollment Confirmation

Successful applicants receive an official acceptance and onboarding guidance.

Admission Requirements

  • ✔ Completed application form
  • ✔ Copy of student birth certificate
  • ✔ Previous school reports
  • ✔ Passport-size photographs
  • ✔ Proof of payment (application fee)
