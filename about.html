Nathan Zingoni watched corridor decisions get made on brochures and optimism — destinations scored by adjective, never by number. Capital was waiting for proof of readiness, and the proof never arrived in a form it could underwrite. Airlines postponed routes. Diaspora capital sat on the sidelines.

The gap wasn't ambition. It was evidence. So he built the instrument to produce it. NSTEDPath exists to make readiness a number, corridors a matter of record, and capital a conversation that can actually close.

Governance & Methodology

Intelligence Methodology Council

Binding Council — 7 Seats

• Chair — Independent

• Independent Advisor (×2)

• Practitioner — Rotating (×2)

• Statistical / Evaluation Seat

• Intelligence Inc. Observer

Quorum Thresholds

• Operational — 3 of 7 voting seats, minimum 2 external. Authors may not approve their own work.

• Constitutional — 5 of 7, minimum 3 external. Required to amend index specifications, weightings, or the charter.

Council Mandate

• Publication ratification — approves all blocks before release; may force revision within 30 days, with original and revised versions published side-by-side.

• Challenge adjudication — issues written decisions within 60 days of any formal challenge.

• Independent review — may commission reviews from a ring-fenced budget without Intelligence sign-off.

• Annual Methodology & Integrity Report — published independently to institutional stakeholders.

⚠

The Council cannot author, edit, price, or select coverage. The 40% coverage floor on any RA is unwaivable — not even by the Council.

Advisory Council — Non-Binding, 13 Seats

8 anchor seats (2 per Intelligence unit lens) + 5 rotating seats (90-day cycles across Hospitality, Infrastructure, Sovereign Capital, and Sustainability). Advisory members co-sign, flag concerns, nominate reviewers, and publish dissent — but hold no veto, no specification authority, and no budget access.
