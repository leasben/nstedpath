About NSTEDPath Investments

We Shape Market Perception —
And Generate Demand.

Based in Los Angeles and operating at the global intersection of Sustainable Tourism and Economic Development, NSTEDPath Investments LLC is a destination marketing intelligence firm.

We provide the digital and economic infrastructure that helps governments, cities, and operators increase visibility, strengthen demand, and sharpen their competitive positioning.

In a market where destination growth is often fragmented by slow consultancy cycles and disconnected data, we have built a destination intelligence platform for the travel industry that turns insight into audience action.

Operational Ecosystem

The Friction-Free Value Chain

We have eliminated the "Contact Us" bottleneck. By bringing intelligence, strategy, and commercial activation under one roof, we create a closed-loop system where every market signal is converted into demand-generating action.

Our Vision

To be the world's leading Destination Marketing Intelligence platform—the company that destinations, DMOs, and governments turn to when they are serious about competing in the global tourism market and winning.

Our Mission

To arm destinations with the market intelligence, demand generation tools, and promotional firepower they need to attract the right travellers, the right investors, and the right trade partners - and to build the ecosystems that make that growth sustainable, measurable, and impossible to ignore.

Executive Leadership

Meet Our Founder

Nathan Zingoni (AvMP), MBA

Nathan Zingoni is a visionary leader in global sustainable tourism development and a pioneer in leveraging artificial intelligence to drive destination vitality. Based in Los Angeles, Nathan specializes at the intersection of business intelligence, aviation, and data analytics to modernize the global tourism ecosystem.

As the founder of NSTEDPath, Nathan developed a suite of AI-powered platforms — The NSTEDPulse, NSTEDConnect, BINDU AI, and MICE Checkpoint. Together, these tools form a comprehensive marketplace that connects destinations with critical services, including event management, specialized research, marketing, and proprietary destination vitality scoring.

His leadership extends to the founding of groundbreaking global initiatives: Tourism Builders Summit, THATS World Networking Summit, APEX Africa Travel Trade Investment Summit, Africa Sport Tourism Excellence Awards (ASTEA), and Africa Sport Tourism Networking Indaba (ASTNI).

Academic & Professional Credentials

Nathan holds a BBA in Marketing Management and an MBA in International Management and Marketing. He is currently pursuing a Doctorate in Business Administration (DBA) focused on Business Intelligence and Data Analytics.

Aviation Expertise

His technical expertise is further bolstered by the IATA Aviation Management Professional (AvMP) designation, providing him with a unique, 360-degree view of the global travel and aviation industry.
