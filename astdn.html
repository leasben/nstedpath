ASTDN

Africa Sport Tourism Development Network

ASTDN is NSTEDPath's dedicated Africa sport tourism vertical — operating under the Networking Engine of THE NSTED PULSE. It is the continent-facing commercial channel that converts Africa's sport tourism opportunity into income through events, partnerships, sponsorship, and sport tourism development mandates. Built on all three NSTEDPath Engines — Networking, Development, and Promotion — ASTDN is designed to design, fund, and activate tourism destinations across Africa. Operating across all 54 African nations, with priority activation markets in East Africa (Rwanda, Kenya, Tanzania), Southern Africa (South Africa, Zimbabwe, Zambia), and West Africa (Ghana, Nigeria, Senegal).

ASTDN Flagship Platforms

Two Platforms. One Mission. Africa's Sport Tourism on the World Stage.

AESTA — Africa Excellence in Sport Tourism Ambassadors Awards

Celebrating excellence and recognising outstanding contributions to sport tourism across Africa. The AESTA Awards Gala is ASTDN's annual flagship revenue activation — generating media value, ministerial attendance, sponsorship income, and partnership conversion. Nominees become clients, sponsors become partners, and winners become brand ambassadors for African sport tourism on the global stage.

Served by: BINDU AI Core 500+ Top Delegates

ASTNI — Africa Sport Tourism Networking Indaba

Africa's premier sport tourism commercial networking platform — bringing together 500+ attendees, 70+ speakers, and 35+ countries. A high-value convening point for sport event rights holders, event managers, parks and recreation personnel, investors, and economic development agencies. ASTNI is where Africa's sport tourism industry sets its agenda, builds its alliances, and unlocks investment and activation pipelines.
